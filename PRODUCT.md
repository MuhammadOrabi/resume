# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Muhammad Orabi is the primary owner and subject of this portfolio. The main audiences are recruiters, hiring managers, and potential freelance clients who need a fast, trustworthy snapshot of technical depth, real-world delivery experience, and communication clarity.

## Product Purpose
This portfolio exists to convert profile visits into real opportunities: interviews, role discussions, and project inquiries. It should make Muhammad's engineering strengths immediately legible, prove practical expertise through concise evidence, and reduce decision friction for people evaluating fit.

## Brand Personality
Geeky, technical, coding-first. The voice should feel competent and builder-oriented, confident without hype, and direct without being cold.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic AI-generated portfolio aesthetics, over-polished agency-like marketing language, and visual noise that hides substance. Avoid playful gimmicks that make the profile feel junior or unfocused.

## Design Principles
1. Show proof before claims: prioritize concrete work signals (skills, projects, outcomes) over abstract self-description.
2. Fast trust gradient: surface identity, specialization, and contact paths early so evaluators can make a quick yes/no judgment.
3. Technical clarity over decoration: interface decisions should improve scanability and comprehension, not distract.
4. Recruiter-speed readability: content must be structured for rapid skim first, deeper read second.
5. Credible personality: retain geek/engineering character while staying professional and opportunity-ready.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, and keyboard access. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid color-only meaning for key information. Keep text readability high on both desktop and mobile with clear hierarchy and generous spacing.
